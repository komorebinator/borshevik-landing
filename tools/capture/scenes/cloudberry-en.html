Cloudberry

The northern berry of bogs and tundra

The cloudberry is a perennial herb that grows in peat bogs, in the tundra and in northern forests. Its berries are red at first and turn soft and amber-yellow as they ripen.

Cloudberries are picked at the end of July and in early August. The berry spoils quickly, so it is made into jam, juice and liqueur right away.

Benefits

Cloudberries are rich in vitamin C, richer than oranges. People of the North have long taken them on long voyages to keep scurvy away.
